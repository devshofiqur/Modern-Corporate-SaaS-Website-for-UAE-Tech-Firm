Every solution we build is crafted with deep respect for UAE's unique business environment — from Arabic-English bilingual systems to VAT compliance and beyond.

AI-Powered Fraud Detection for Major UAE Bank

The Challenge

The bank was losing AED 15M+ annually to fraudulent transactions. Legacy rule-based systems missed 40% of fraud while generating excessive false positives.

Our Solution

Built a real-time ML pipeline using ensemble models, graph neural networks for account relationship analysis, and an explainable AI layer for regulatory compliance.

The Results

94% fraud reduction, AED 12M saved annually, 78% reduction in false positives, and full CBUAE compliance. ROI achieved in under 4 months.
